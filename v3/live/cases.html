How it works

From first call to the right attorney.

We are not a law firm. We are carbon monoxide specialists who help you understand what happened, and connect you with an attorney who has handled cases like yours.

  1. Tell us what happened

    Online in five minutes, or by phone. Where, when, symptoms and any records you have.

  2. Specialist review

    Your case is reviewed against over 25 years of carbon monoxide cases. You hear back within one business day.

  3. The right attorney

    If there is a case, we introduce you to a partner attorney experienced in carbon monoxide litigation in your state.

  4. Expert support

    Medical and technical experts from our network can help your attorney prove what happened.
