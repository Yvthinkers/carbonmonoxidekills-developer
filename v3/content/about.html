Why this site exists

Help for everyone who has been harmed.

Carbon Monoxide Kills was created to help everyone understand the dangers of carbon monoxide: what it is, what it does to the body, and what to do when it has already caused harm.

Since 1999, Rob has built a team of medical and legal experts who answer detailed medical questions and support people in legal battles against whoever exposed them. We work with partner attorneys throughout the United States, so whether you are in New York or Alaska, we can help, including with workers’ comp.
