Clinical reminders

  • Ask about the setting. Several people or pets ill at once, symptoms that ease away from home, or worse when heating runs.
  • Test early. COHb falls quickly with fresh air or oxygen. Record the time of the sample and of the last exposure.
  • Don’t rely on pulse oximetry. Standard SpO₂ readings can look normal in significant poisoning.
  • Plan follow-up. Delayed neurological sequelae can appear days to weeks after apparent recovery.
