The short answer

In brief

In Michigan you generally have three years to file a personal injury claim for carbon monoxide poisoning. A wrongful death claim follows the same period, and a family can get extra time once a personal representative of the estate is appointed. Claims against the state or a local government have short written-notice rules of their own.

Hotels, motels and boarding houses built since December 1, 2009 must have a carbon monoxide device at each furnace, boiler or water heater area, and new homes need alarms under the state building code.

Michigan time limits

Injury: 3 yearsFrom the date of the poisoning, in most cases (MCL 600.5805).
Wrongful deathGenerally the same three years, with extra time after the estate representative is appointed (MCL 600.5852).
Government bodiesWritten notice is often due within months. Get advice early if a public body may be involved.
ExceptionsChildren and some other cases follow different rules. Have a lawyer confirm your date.

Carbon monoxide alarms in Michigan

  • Hotels, motels and boarding houses built on or after December 1, 2009: one working carbon monoxide device at each source point, meaning an area served by a fossil-fuel furnace, boiler or water heater (MCL 125.1504d).
  • New homes: carbon monoxide alarms under the Michigan Residential Code, which the state adopts under the same construction code act.
  • Older homes and rentals may not have been required to have one. Local housing codes can add rules.

An owner who installs and maintains a device as the maker instructs is protected from claims about the device itself, but not from claims about the heating system that produced the gas.

What we see in Michigan

Furnaces running for half the year, older homes and flats with aging boilers and chimneys, cars warmed up in attached garages, ice fishing shanties and cabins heated by propane, and generators during winter power cuts.

Questions

How long do I have in Michigan?

Usually three years from the poisoning. Families bringing a wrongful death claim may have more time once an estate representative is appointed, but do not wait to find out.

We were poisoned in a motel. Did it need a detector?

If it was built on or after December 1, 2009, Michigan law required one at each furnace, boiler or water heater area. Older buildings may have other duties under local codes.
